# TRUE NORTH - MISSION SYSTEM

## Philosophy

A Mission is not a goal.

A Mission is a period of focused effort that moves me toward the man I want to become.

I only ever have ONE Current Mission.

Every Mission exists to strengthen one area of my life.

---

## Mission Structure

Mission Name

Mission Purpose

Why this matters

Success Criteria

Current Progress

Next Milestone

Mission Status

Lessons Learned

Evidence Produced

---

## Dashboard

The Current Mission should always be visible.

It should answer:

What am I building right now?

Why does it matter?

What is my next step?

---

## Completed Missions

Completed Missions become a permanent record of growth.

They are never deleted.

They become proof.

---

## Mission Status

Every Mission has one status.

The user does not enter free text.

Instead they select one predefined status.

Statuses:

🟢 ON TRACK

Progress is being made consistently.

No intervention required.

---

🟡 AT RISK

Progress has slowed.

The mission requires attention.

---

🔴 OFF COURSE

The mission has stalled.

Immediate course correction is required.

---

⚪ COMPLETE

The mission has been successfully completed.

It will move into Completed Missions.

---
