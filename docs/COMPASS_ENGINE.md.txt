# TRUE NORTH - COMPASS ENGINE

## Purpose

The Compass is the centrepiece of True North.

It is not a score.

It is not a statistic.

It answers one question:

"Am I becoming the man I said I would become?"

---

## Philosophy

The Compass measures alignment.

Not achievement.

Not productivity.

Alignment with My Standard.

The user should never chase numbers.

The user should chase direction.

---

## Compass States

🟢 TRUE NORTH

Living consistently according to My Standard.

---

🟡 DRIFTING

Small inconsistencies are appearing.

A course correction is recommended.

---

🟠 OFF COURSE

Recent actions no longer consistently align with My Standard.

Immediate attention is recommended.

---

🔴 LOST

Significant drift has occurred.

Return to My Standard.

One step at a time.

---

## Dashboard

The Compass is the largest element on the dashboard.

It displays:

Compass

Heading

One sentence of guidance

Example

Stay the course.

---

## Alignment Report

When the Compass is tapped it opens a full report.

The report contains:

Current Heading

Strongest Standard

Greatest Opportunity

Current Drift

Suggested Course Correction

Mission Alignment

Recent Progress

Upcoming Focus
