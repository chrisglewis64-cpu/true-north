# TRUE NORTH - COMPASS ENGINE

## Purpose

The Compass is the emotional centre of True North.

It is the living representation of identity and alignment.

It answers:

"How aligned are my actions with my standards?"

"Am I becoming the person I committed to be?"

---

## Identity Alignment

Identity Alignment is a measure of alignment between identity and behaviour — not productivity.

Displayed as 0–100% on the instrument panel, with a seven-day trend.

Never use gamified language: Score, XP, Level, Streak, Points.

---

## Calculation

Implemented in `lib/identity-alignment/` — isolated from UI so weights can change without touching Compass components.

### Day score (equal pillars)

1. Morning Commitment completed
2. Mission progress
3. Evidence recorded
4. Daily Debrief completed

### Temporal horizons

| Horizon | Weight | Window |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Today's Alignment | 20% | Current local day |
| Recent Alignment | 50% | Previous 30 local days (missing days = 0) |
| Long-Term Character | 30% | Lifetime span from first activity |

A single bad day barely moves the composite. Weeks of inconsistency produce visible drift. Lifetime changes slowly.

---

## Compass Behaviour

| Alignment | Bearing |
|-----------|---------|
| 95–100% | Near True North |
| 85–94% | Very slight drift |
| 70–84% | Noticeable drift |
| 50–69% | Clearly off course |
| Below 50% | Significantly misaligned |

Needle movement is subtle and elegant.

---

## Instrument Panel

After Morning Commitment, the Compass is the first thing users see.

It displays:

- Compass dial + needle
- Identity Alignment %
- Heading label (e.g. Living Your Standard)
- Weekly trend (▲ / ▼)
- Identity Insight — one coaching sentence (why + next action)

---

## Identity Insight

Active coach beneath Identity Alignment.

Rule-based today (`lib/identity-insight/`); provider interface allows AI later without UI changes.

Tone: trusted mentor — positive, constructive, action-oriented. Never shame or guilt.

Focus priority: Morning Commitment → Debrief → Evidence → Mission → reinforce when strong.
